Class report

Ask each learner to download their completion record (Final assessment → Download completion record) and send you the .json file. Drop the whole batch here to see who has met all three criteria, which units the class found hardest, and to export the results. Combined Passport records from the hub also work.

1 · Add records

Drop completion-record files here (you can drop many at once)

    Nothing leaves this device. The files are read by this page in your browser; there is no server and nothing is uploaded. Close the tab and the report is gone — export the CSV if you want to keep it. Records are self-attested: a matching checksum shows a record was not casually altered, not that the learner sat the assessment. Use this for teaching and feedback, not for high-stakes decisions.

    3 · Where the class struggled

    How to read this. Right first time is the share of first-ever answers that were correct — what the class knew before the unit taught them. Misses counts every wrong checkpoint answer, including repeats. Confident & wrong counts items a learner answered “sure” and got wrong and has not yet corrected — the misconceptions most worth a teaching session. Exam misses are wrong answers in the final assessment attributed to that unit. Units are sorted with the weakest first.
